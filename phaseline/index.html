PHASELINE Support

PHASELINE is an immersive rhythm game for Apple Vision Pro.

Getting started

Choose a track, difficulty and arena, then select Play. Allow hand tracking when prompted. Punch red crystals with your left hand and blue crystals with your right; push flat-hand plates with an open hand. Give yourself enough clear space to move comfortably.

Your music

Choose a local audio file or a compatible rhythm-map ZIP from Files. Use content you have the rights to. DRM-protected Apple Music tracks cannot be imported.

Need help?

Contact PHASELINE support on GitHub. Issues are public, so please don’t include private information. Include your visionOS version and a short description of the problem.
