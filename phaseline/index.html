Support

Get playing in the arena or in your room on Apple Vision Pro.

Start a track

Choose a track, an arena and a difficulty. Allow hand tracking if asked. Punch red crystals with your left hand and blue with your right. Use an open hand for flat plates. Hold a pinch with both hands to pause.

Play in your room

Choose Nova Room, Room Runway, or Room Pulse and allow World Sensing. Look around for a moment, then face an open area and select Start. When the room scan finds a clear wall, a portal opens there and sends crystals toward you. In Nova Room, wall lights pulse with the beat, crystals reflect the room’s lighting, and sparks bounce off mapped furniture and floors. Clear enough space to move your arms and stay in place while playing.

If a nearby surface blocks the runway, face an open area and select Check Room. You can manage World Sensing access in Settings on Apple Vision Pro.

Bring your own music

In Your Music, choose an audio file from Files or a compatible rhythm-map ZIP that you created or have permission to use. Encrypted Apple Music tracks cannot be imported.

Get in touch

Include your visionOS version, the arena and what happened. Support issues are public, so leave out private information.
