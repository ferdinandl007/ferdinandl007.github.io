PHASELINE Support

Privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026

PHASELINE is a rhythm game for Apple Vision Pro. It does not require an account or include advertising, tracking or analytics services.

Hand tracking and room sensing

PHASELINE processes hand positions on your device to control the game. If you select a mixed reality arena and allow World Sensing, the app uses a mesh of nearby surfaces to position game objects, place a portal on a suitable wall, hide virtual objects behind real surfaces, and show effects where sparks touch those surfaces. The app does not save or transmit the room mesh or hand tracking data. You can revoke these permissions in Apple Vision Pro Settings.

Files, scores and settings

If you select an audio file or rhythm-map archive, the app reads it on your device to prepare and play the selected content. Scores and preferences are saved on your device. The app does not upload your music or maps.

Support

If you contact support through GitHub, your issue and any information you include may be public and are subject to GitHub’s privacy practices. For questions about this policy, use the support page.
