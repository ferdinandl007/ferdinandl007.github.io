PHASELINE Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

PHASELINE is a rhythm game for Apple Vision Pro. The release app does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics services.

Hand tracking

PHASELINE uses hand-tracking input to control gameplay. The app processes that input on your device and does not send it to a PHASELINE server.

Files and settings

If you choose an audio file or map from Files, PHASELINE reads it to prepare and play the selected content. Settings and scores are stored on your device.

Support

If you contact us through GitHub, your issue and the information you include may be publicly visible and are subject to GitHub’s privacy practices. Please do not include sensitive information in a public issue.

For questions, visit PHASELINE Support.
