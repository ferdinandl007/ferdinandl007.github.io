Paste Muse Support

Paste Muse is a menu-bar clipboard history for macOS.

Find a recent copy

Enable clipboard history in the welcome guide or Settings. Copy something in another app, then press ⌘⇧P or open Paste Muse from the menu bar. Select an item to copy it back. In Settings, you can change the shortcut, manage retention, or allow optional direct pasting.

Get help

Open a support issue. Include the macOS version, Paste Muse version, and the steps you took. Leave clipboard contents and credentials out of public reports.
